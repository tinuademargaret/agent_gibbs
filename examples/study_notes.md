Q: What is entropy?
A: A measure of uncertainty in a random variable.
Explanation: Entropy measures the average surprise in an outcome.
For a fair coin, both outcomes are equally likely, so entropy is 1 bit.

## 2. What is a derivative?
**Answer:** The instantaneous rate of change of a function.
**Detailed explanation:** The derivative of position with respect to time gives velocity.
For position x(t) = t², velocity is 2t.

Q: What is retrieval practice?
A: Trying to recall information from memory.
Explanation: Think through the question before revealing the answer.
Then compare what you remembered with the saved source material.
